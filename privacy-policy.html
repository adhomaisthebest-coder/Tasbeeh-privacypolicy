Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Tasbeeh ("the App") is an Islamic counter application developed for personal dhikr and prayer tracking. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles data.

1. Data Collection

The App itself does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required.
  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number) are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No camera, microphone, or contact data is accessed.

All counter data, session statistics, and dhikr preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Appodeal mediation, which works with multiple third-party ad networks. These ad networks may collect device-level information such as:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Advertising identifier (GAID)
  • IP address (for approximate geolocation and fraud prevention)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected by the ad networks, not by the App developer. For details on how individual ad networks handle data, please refer to the Appodeal Privacy Policy.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID).

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements. This transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The App developer does not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Google account data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

5. Data Storage and Security

All application data (counters, sessions, statistics) is stored locally on your device using Android's built-in database systems. This data does not leave your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
